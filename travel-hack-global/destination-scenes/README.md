# Travel Hack Global — Destination Scene References

Example aspirational travel imagery for use as visual register references in ad creative and funnels.

**Public URL pattern:**
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/contextarchitect/brand-assets/main/travel-hack-global/destination-scenes/[filename]`

## Purpose

These images define the visual register for Travel Hack creative. They are NOT generated by AI — they are examples provided by the client or sourced by the creative team to anchor what the brand's aspirational imagery should look and feel like. Nano Banana prompts reference these when generating lifestyle scenes.

## Asset Categories and REF Ranges

| REF Range | Category | Avatar Match | Examples |
|-----------|----------|--------------|----------|
| REF-010 to REF-014 | Urban/architectural | All avatars | City skylines, rooftop views, distinctive streets, neighbourhood scenes |
| REF-015 to REF-019 | Nature/landscape | Priya, Jess | Coastal, mountain, tropical landscape |
| REF-020 to REF-024 | Interior ambience | All avatars | Unbranded hotel room interiors, lobby atmosphere |
| REF-025 to REF-029 | Lifestyle moments | All avatars | People in travel contexts, exploring, on terraces |

## Visual Rules for All Images in This Folder

- NO real hotel names, logos, branded signage, or identifiable branded facades
- NO generic stock-photo resort pools or posed “happy family on beach” tropes
- NO luxury branding or aspirational imagery that reads as a hotel brochure
- Focus: the destination, not the property
- People shown must reflect the avatar demographic: 32–55, US-diverse, doing things rather than posing

## Naming Convention

Name files descriptively so their content is clear without opening them:
- `lisbon-cityscape-01.jpg`
- `hotel-room-interior-family-01.jpg`
- `coastal-landscape-priya-01.jpg`

## Upload Instructions

Drag and drop files into this folder via GitHub UI. Commit to `main`.
Notify the operator once uploaded so REF codes can be assigned in `image-reference-index.md`.

**Format requirements:** JPG or PNG, minimum 1500px on the longest edge, landscape orientation preferred for ad creative use.
